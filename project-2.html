Project Documentation Template

Research / Prototyping / Final Build

Project overview

This template is designed for a simple project documentation page. Each section captures a phase of the process, from initial research to the final result, making it easy to document ideas, prototypes, and completed work.

Stage 1

Concept and brief

Define the goal, target audience, and constraints. Gather inspiration, note the materials, and outline the first sketch or concept direction.

Stage 2

Research and references

Review relevant examples and collect visual references. This helps establish the design language and guides the next steps before constructing the prototype.

Stage 3

Prototype

Build a first version to test proportions, function, and assembly. Capture problems and improvements so the next iteration is more accurate and efficient.

Stage 4

Testing and iteration

Assess the prototype against the original goals. Small changes in shape, material, or size can make a big difference in performance and appearance.

Stage 5

Final result

Present the completed project with the final materials, dimensions, and a short explanation of what was learned. Include imagery that highlights the finished outcome.

What I learned

Write a brief reflection here: what worked well, what could be improved, and how the process evolved from concept to final build. This gives the documentation a clear conclusion and helps future projects build on the same process.
